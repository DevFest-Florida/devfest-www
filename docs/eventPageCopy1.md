# **DevFest Florida Hackathon**

## **Build, Innovate & Connect**
**November 7–8, 2026 · UCF Downtown · Orlando, Florida**
Join the DevFest Florida Hackathon for a weekend where developers, designers, data practitioners, game creators, product thinkers, storytellers, and curious problem-solvers come together to imagine, build, and share. Bring an idea, bring a skill, or simply bring your curiosity. There is a place for you on this team.
This is more than a competition. It is a community-powered opportunity to learn new tools, meet generous collaborators, and create technology that reflects human creativity, care, and purpose.
**[Explore the schedule]**

## **Find your people and build something meaningful**
You do not need to arrive with a team or a fully formed idea. We will create time and space for you to meet collaborators, discover shared interests, and shape a project together.
Throughout the weekend, you can:
* Experiment with Data science, Game Dev, Communication Tech, AI App Development, Flutter, Cloud technologies, WebXR.
* Learn how to use AI responsibly as a partner for human creativity and problem-solving.
* Work across disciplines with people who bring different perspectives and strengths.
* Get practical guidance from mentors and experienced engineering leaders.
* Meet potential employers, future teammates, collaborators, and co-founders.
* Turn an early idea into a prototype you can share with the community.

## **Your ticket supports the whole experience**
We want you focused on learning, building, and connecting. Your ticket includes:
* Breakfast, lunches, dinner, and snacks during scheduled event activities.
* Hands-on workshops that support your professional growth and your hackathon project.
* Access to mentors throughout the build.
* Team-formation and project-planning support.
* A welcoming community of builders, creators, and technology enthusiasts.
* Opportunities to connect with employers and engineering leaders.
* The chance to present your project, compete for prizes, and celebrate together.

## **Choose a track that feels like you**
Bring your interests to one of three tracks. Each track welcomes different skills, and every strong team needs more than one kind of builder.

### **Devs Track**
Build useful software with APIs, web or mobile applications, cloud infrastructure, machine learning, or other technologies that solve real-world problems.

### **Game Devs Track**
Create playable prototypes and interactive media with gameplay programming, technical art, sound, animation, and creative experimentation.

### **Data for Good Track**
Use open data, analytics, and clear visualizations to address community challenges such as civic accessibility, education, and learning opportunities.

## **A weekend to learn, build, and celebrate**

### **Friday, November 6 — Come as you are**
**6:30–8:30 PM · Hackathon social kickoff**
Start the weekend by meeting the community, learning about the themes and tracks, and finding potential teammates. Come with a project idea or come ready to discover one with others.
Location for hackathon social is still in the works.

### **Saturday, November 7 — Learn, plan, and build**
The day begins with orientation and a keynote, followed by workshops in WebXR, agentic engineering, Flutter, AI, architecture, and Gemini. After planning and team alignment, the hack begins at **2:30 PM**. We will share dinner, keep building, and wrap up the first day at **8:00 PM**.

### **Sunday, November 8 — Share what you made**
Enjoy breakfast, continue building, and submit your project by **3:30 PM**. Teams will present from **3:30–5:30 PM**, followed by judge deliberations, awards, and an after-party with the community.
View the full schedule before you arrive so you can plan your weekend.
