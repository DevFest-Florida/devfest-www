## Why attend

DevFest Florida is more than a coding competition. It is a collaborative space to:

- Experiment with AI, Flutter, cloud technologies, WebXR, game development, and data.
- Build practical solutions with people who bring different skills and perspectives.
- Learn responsible and ethical ways to use AI as a tool for human creativity and problem-solving.
- Get feedback from mentors, engineering leaders, and experienced builders.
- Meet potential employers, future teammates, collaborators, and co-founders.
- Leave with new skills, a project to showcase, and a stronger local tech network.

No team yet? No problem. Saturday includes structured hackathon planning so you can meet collaborators and form a balanced team.

## Featured learning experiences

Saturday’s workshops are designed to be immediately useful during the hackathon:

- **Dwane Hemmings, JavaScript Developer Advocate at Vonage**  
  *WebXR: Augment Reality with Real-time Communications* — Build a WebXR application capable of receiving phone calls in an interactive code-along workshop.

- **Will Gorman, CTO at Nucleus Security**  
  *Agentic Engineering Workshop* — Explore how agent skills and agent orchestration are changing the software development life cycle.

- **Carol Bolger, Founder and Flutter & AI Consultant**  
  *Ask My Data: Building Grounded Gemini Agents with Flutter & Firebase* — Build a Flutter chat app that uses live Firestore data to ground Gemini responses.

- **Javiera Laso, Lead Developer Consultant at Thoughtworks**  
  *Build Your Own AI-Assisted Architecture Review Board* — Create a practical blueprint for using AI to make architectural guidance more accessible, consistent, and scalable while preserving human judgment.

## Choose your track

Teams can focus their project on one of three tracks:

### Devs Track

Build useful software with APIs, web or mobile applications, cloud infrastructure, machine learning, or other technologies that solve real-world problems.

### Game Devs Track

Create playable prototypes and interactive media with gameplay programming, technical art, sound, animation, and creative experimentation.

### Data for Good Track

Use open data, analytics, and clear visualizations to address community challenges such as civic accessibility, education, and learning opportunities.
