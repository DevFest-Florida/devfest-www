# **Join the Data for Good Applied Research Sprint at DevFest Florida 2026**
**November 7–8, 2026 · UCF Downtown, Orlando · In person**
If you've ever wanted a weekend where the hard part of the problem is the fun part, this is your invitation.
The **Data for Good track** at DevFest Hackathon 2026 is an **applied research sprint** focused on one question: *how can data help students succeed?* You get two days to frame a real problem in education, test a hypothesis against well-documented public data, and leave with a reproducible result you're proud to put your name on. The event is hosted by GDG Central Florida, with partners including Google and DataPsy.
Whether you're a senior practitioner or still building your ML intuition, we'd love to have you in the room.

## **Start With the Data**
We know you want to see the schema before you see the swag. Every dataset below is **public**, and several are synthetically augmented. Treat them as a starting point. You're welcome to join, augment, or challenge them with other public sources.

### **🎯 Theme 1: Catch students before they slip**
*Early-warning models that flag students who need support while there's still time to help.*
**Classification with an Academic Success Dataset** ([Kaggle Playground S4E6](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/playground-series-s4e6))
* **Records:** 76,518 students (synthetically augmented from real higher-ed cohorts)
* **Dimensionality:** 36 features covering demographics, parental education and occupation, socioeconomic indicators, and 1st/2nd-semester credit metrics
* **Granularity:** One row per student, with two semester snapshots
* **Task:** 3-class classification (Dropout / Enrolled / Graduate), scored on accuracy
* **Edge cases:** Synthetic-augmentation artifacts, and a hard boundary between *Enrolled* and *Dropout*. Early curricular momentum (approved vs. enrolled units, tuition status) outweighs static background traits.
* **License:** CC BY 4.0
**Open University Learning Analytics Dataset (OULAD)** ([Kaggle](https://doi.org/10.34740/kaggle/dsv/8949892))
* **Records:** 32,593 distance learners across 22 course presentations (2013–2014), plus **10.6M clickstream interactions**
* **Dimensionality:** 7 relational tables linking demographics, registrations, assessments, and VLE activity
* **Granularity:** Daily click counts per learning resource, indexed to module start date
* **Task:** Binary dropout prediction, or 4-class final result (Distinction / Pass / Fail / Withdrawn). F1 and ROC-AUC are typical metrics.
* **Edge cases:** Irregular time-series sampling, students who withdraw mid-module, and multi-table joins that reward careful temporal feature aggregation
* **License:** CC BY 4.0
**xAPI-Edu-Data** ([Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/aljarah/xAPI-Edu-Data))
* **Records:** 480 K–12 students
* **Dimensionality:** 17 behavioral, academic, and demographic attributes (hand-raises, resource visits, discussion posts, parent survey responses)
* **Granularity:** One row per student per semester
* **Task:** Ordinal 3-class performance band (Low / Medium / High), scored on accuracy and macro F1
* **Edge cases:** Small *n*, gender imbalance (305 male / 175 female), and a multinational sample. Overfitting is the real opponent here.
* **License:** Open access (check the source page)

### **💬 Theme 2: Tutors that teach, not tell**
*AI tutoring that guides students to the answer instead of handing it over.*
**MathDial** ([Hugging Face: eth-nlped/mathdial](https://huggingface.co/datasets/eth-nlped/mathdial))
* **Records:** ~2,860 annotated multi-turn tutoring dialogues
* **Schema:** Dialogue turns, the student's misconception, problem context (from GSM8K), and gold solution steps
* **Granularity:** Per conversational turn
* **Task:** Socratic dialogue generation and pedagogical alignment
* **Edge cases:** "Solution handover," where the tutor simply gives the answer away. The challenge is scaffolding without telling, and fading support at the right moment.
* **License:** MIT / CC BY 4.0
**TutorChat** ([Hugging Face: princeton-nlp/TutorChat](https://huggingface.co/datasets/princeton-nlp/TutorChat))
* **Records:** 77,300 synthetic tutoring dialogues grounded in open textbooks (STEM and humanities)
* **Schema:** Multi-turn exchanges, learner personas, topical context, and open-book vs. closed-book constraints
* **Task:** Instruction tuning for educational dialogue
* **Edge cases:** Synthetic persona artifacts and uneven conversational depth across subjects
* **License:** See the dataset card
**Want more?** Our full catalog also includes ASAP-AES, SciQ, RACE, and StudyChat.[ Browse the complete dataset guide →](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vTnVdfGfUPnhRk2RbUz7SFByfmfNU3N62_Cu0LHYJXRcUq3gcUlnHfs7M72Y8-6V9ZFpUWSIGH7Xgqp/pub)
**A note on responsible use:** These datasets describe real students, even when they're anonymized or synthetically expanded. We encourage teams to report subgroup performance, document limitations, and design for students' benefit rather than surveillance.

## **Why Join?**

### **Intellectual rigor**
This isn't a race to the flashiest demo. It's a chance to get the method right: clear problem framing, sound validation, honest uncertainty, and fairness checks across student groups. Mentors will be on hand to push your thinking.

### **Peer networking**
Spend three days working alongside data scientists, ML engineers, analysts, and education experts from Central Florida and beyond. Come solo and join Friday's team-formation session, or bring a team of 2 to 5.

### **Kaggle Notebooks

**Kaggle offers generous, consistent free access without charging for compute.
1. **Verify your account:** Complete phone number verification in your Kaggle profile settings (required to unlock accelerator access).
2. **Create a notebook:** Navigate to **Code** > **New Notebook**.
3. **Turn on the GPU:** In the right-hand **Notebook Options** panel:
 * Expand **Accelerator**.
 * Choose **GPU T4 x2** or **GPU P100**.
