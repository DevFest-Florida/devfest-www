DevFest Florida Hackathon is made possible by thoughtful developers, community organizers, and volunteers.   DevFest is organized for the good of the community by a community of volunteers.   We wanted to take a moment to express our thanks.  Many of our volunteers have dedicated hours of time to help ensure we have a great experience.  

It's been inspiring to hear our community organizers reflect on ways we can create empowering, fun, and helpful experiences through our meetup groups and DevFest.  We collectively desire a world where 
developers, designers, and builders can join forces/code to positively impact the world.  We all feel a sense that the world needs authentic human connection and community more than ever. We all have a shared mission to empower the next generation of great builders, startups and businesses. 

Big high fives to our tribe of organizers, volunteers, speakers, and meetup leaders.  You are making a difference!

### Community Support 

We are thankful for the community support of our fellow dev and product meetup groups in Orlando.  

-   **ODevs (Orlando Devs)**
    
    Orlando Devs is Central Florida’s largest general software developer community, bringing together engineers, designers, and tech enthusiasts for regular social events, hackathons, and technical talks. Its primary impact is serving as the foundational grassroots hub for the region's tech ecosystem, helping local talent find mentorship, discover job opportunities, and build lasting professional connections.
    
-   **Space Coast Devs**
    Operating just east of Orlando along the Space Coast, this community connects programmers, aerospace technologists, designers, and IT professionals to share knowledge across modern software practices. It impacts the broader Central Florida corridor by bridging the gap between Orlando’s digital tech talent and the specialized defense, aerospace, and commercial space industries.

-   **BOT (Building Our Tech)**
    BOT is a nonprofit and community initiative dedicated to driving economic mobility and closing diversity gaps in Central Florida's innovation economy. Through technical training, mentorship, and startup resources, it empowers underrepresented professionals to launch tech careers and build sustainable, high-growth businesses.
    
-   **Orlando JUG (Java User Group)**
    
    Orlando JUG is a specialized technical meetup dedicated to the Java ecosystem, covering frameworks like Spring and Quarkus alongside emerging trends in cloud and AI integration. By hosting in-depth architectural sessions and hands-on coding demos, it elevates enterprise software standards across the region and keeps local developers aligned with industry best practices.

-   **Orlando Innovation League (OIL)**
    The Orlando Innovation League convenes founders, innovators, technologists, and investors through discussions, pitch events, and collaborative workshops. Its impact lies in catalyzing Central Florida’s entrepreneurial culture, transforming early-stage ideas into viable ventures and fostering partnerships across the local startup ecosystem.

-   **ProductTank Orlando**
    Affiliated with the global Mind the Product network, ProductTank Orlando brings together product managers, UX designers, and tech leaders to exchange product design and strategy insights. It elevates the local tech economy by maturing product-led thinking, ensuring Central Florida companies build user-centric, scalable products rather than just writing code.

-   **indienomicon.org**
    Indienomicon is a community-driven organization dedicated to showcasing, supporting, and accelerating indie game development and interactive digital media throughout Central Florida. It has shaped Orlando’s creative tech scene by providing indie developers with playtesting stages, game jams, and direct pipelines to commercial visibility and industry collaboration.

-   **ONETUG (Orlando .NET User Group)**
    ONETUG is a community focused on Microsoft development tools, C#, Azure, and the evolving open-source .NET ecosystem. Its long-standing presence provides vital enterprise-grade training, upskilling local engineers to meet the demands of Central Florida's prominent corporate and healthcare employers.
