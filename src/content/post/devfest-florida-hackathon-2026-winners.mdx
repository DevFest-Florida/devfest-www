## 📊 DataKind Track

In this track, participants leveraged community data to tackle local socioeconomic disparities and food access across Central Florida. 

[DataKind](https://www.datakind.org/) supported two hackathon challenges featuring [Beacon](https://www.marketing.explorebeacon.org/), its community data platform, and [GUIDE](https://guide.datakind.org/), its open-source initiative advancing higher education data interoperability. Participants used Beacon's public community indicators and GUIDE's shared higher education data model to explore practical applications in community development and student success.

### 🥇 1st Place: No Child Left Hungry

* **Project Summary:** An interactive, school-centered decision-support tool built with Next.js 16, React 19, MapLibre GL, and Tailwind CSS. It links Florida Department of Education (FLDOE) school data with census-tract poverty and SNAP retailer access across 139 elementary schools in Orange County, Florida. Using seven transparent, explainable indicators, the tool ranks schools and offers a four-step workflow (Identify, Investigate, Verify, Reach Out) so food banks can identify priority partner schools without guessing.


* **Why It Matters & Impact:** Food banks often have the resources to launch school nutrition programs, but deciding *which* school needs assistance next is a massive challenge. Community-level poverty stats don't always reflect the exact student body of a school, and raw school metrics miss neighborhood food deserts. By bridging this gap, *No Child Left Hungry* replaces guesswork with traceable, actionable evidence—helping food-assistance programs reach vulnerable kids before nutrition gaps impact their learning and well-being.


* 🔗 **View on Devpost:** [Devpost Project Gallery – No Child Left Hungry](https://devfest-hackathon-2026.devpost.com/project-gallery)

### 🥈 2nd Place: SNAP GAP

* **Project Summary:** A real-time community assistance web platform powered by Vite and Firebase. SNAP GAP directly assists individuals facing difficulties navigating SNAP benefit qualification guidelines, offering guidance on eligibility, nearby food pantry locations, and community-driven meal delivery coordination.


* **Why It Matters & Impact:** As qualification thresholds shift, families frequently slip through administrative cracks. SNAP GAP focuses on immediate intervention—ensuring no family is left without support while navigating social safety net programs.


* 🔗 **View on Devpost:** [Devpost Project Gallery – SNAP GAP](https://devfest-hackathon-2026.devpost.com/project-gallery)

### 🥉 3rd Place: Nurish

* **Project Summary:** A community grocery-request and logistics suite built using Vite and Firebase. Nurish allows families in need to request food drop-offs and enables volunteers—including those seeking service hours or driving nearby—to pick up and coordinate grocery deliveries. It also features a dedicated logistics portal for local food banks to balance supply, manage available inventory, and route trucks closer to communities.


* **Why It Matters & Impact:** Getting to a physical food bank is often hindered by lack of transportation, work hours, or mobility issues. Nurish tackles the "last-mile" delivery problem of food assistance, leveraging community drivers and streamlined food-bank logistics to bring balanced meals straight to doorsteps.


* 🔗 **View on Devpost:** [Devpost Project Gallery – Nurish](https://devfest-hackathon-2026.devpost.com/project-gallery)

Players locate countries from flags, exports, or trivia, spin a low-poly 3D globe to catch falling supply crates, and climb the leaderboard through an adaptive clue ladder.

## 📱 Apps Track

The Apps Track highlighted cutting-edge engineering—from runtime security for AI agents to accessible mobile tools and multimodal cooking companions.

### 🥇 1st Place: ShadowMCP



* **Project Summary:** An open-source, tool-call security firewall for AI agents using the Model Context Protocol (MCP). ShadowMCP sits between an autonomous agent and its tools to stop indirect prompt injection and "confused deputy" attacks. Using a two-stage routing pipeline (fast local heuristic rules + an LLM judge), it classifies actions into SAFE, REVIEW (paused for human approval), or HONEYPOT (feeding synthetic canary credentials and sinkholing exfiltration without alerting the hijacked agent). It includes a full Streamlit SOC dashboard, an MCP stdio proxy, and Python decorators.


* **Why It Matters & Impact:** As autonomous AI agents gain access to shell environments, internal databases, and private APIs, indirect prompt injection represents one of the largest security threats in generative AI. Poisoned documents or web pages can covertly trick agents into stealing API keys or wiping databases. ShadowMCP solves this vulnerability at the tool boundary—protecting enterprise infrastructure while maintaining human oversight.


* 🔗 **View on Devpost:** [Devpost Project Gallery – ShadowMCP](https://devfest-hackathon-2026.devpost.com/project-gallery)

### 🥈 2nd Place: Pitch Practice

* **Project Summary:** A clean, native iOS application built with Swift, SwiftUI, AVFoundation, and Core ML / WhisperKit. Tailored for vocalists and choir singers, it plays reference pitches, listens to and detects sung notes in real time, and allows singers to record, replay, and critique their rehearsal sessions.


* **Why It Matters & Impact:** Private vocal coaching and specialized pitch equipment are expensive and often inaccessible. *Pitch Practice* provides singers of all experience levels with instant visual feedback right on their iPhones, building vocal confidence and ear-training habits without friction or fees.


* 🔗 **View on Devpost:** [Devpost Project Gallery – Pitch Practice](https://devfest-hackathon-2026.devpost.com/project-gallery)

### 🥉 3rd Place: SousSight

* **Project Summary:** A live, multimodal cooking companion web app built with Flutter Web, GenUI/A2UI, and Firebase. Powered by Gemini Live (for real-time hands-free voice and counter camera checks) and Gemini Flash (for ingredient recognition, recipe generation, and Google Maps/Search grounding), SousSight lets users show whatever random items are on their counter, generates an adaptive recipe, watches preparation technique, checks food safety and allergens, and texts household members when dinner is ready via the Vonage Messages API.


* **Why It Matters & Impact:** When students or families receive food assistance from pantries or community gardens, the biggest hurdle is often knowing what to make with unfamiliar, mismatched ingredients. SousSight turns pantry staples into nutritious, complete meals while boosting kitchen confidence—reducing food waste and helping home cooks feel supported every step of the way.


* 🔗 **View on Devpost:** [Devpost Project Gallery – SousSight](https://devfest-hackathon-2026.devpost.com/project-gallery)
