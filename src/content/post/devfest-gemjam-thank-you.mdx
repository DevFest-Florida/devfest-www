# What an Incredible Journey! Thank You for DevFest GemJam 2025!

On behalf of the entire DevFest Florida organizing team, I want to extend our deepest, most heartfelt thanks to every single person who made DevFest GemJam 2025 an unforgettable experience.

This year, we embarked on a new adventure: a week-long journey into the world of Generative AI. We called it "DevFest GemJam," and our goal was to create a welcoming space for collaboration, learning, and building the future with AI. We watched this community come together to imagine, build, and share incredible ideas, focusing not just on technology, but on creativity, community impact, and responsible innovation.

From the kickoff and workshops at Seminole State College to the intense week of building and iterating, and finally, the amazing projects showcased on Demo Day... your passion and energy were the driving force behind it all. We are so proud to be part of a community that is helping to grow the next generation of technology professionals and spark new ideas right here in Florida.

Make sure to check out [our pictures](https://photos.app.goo.gl/C1TSoPzW5LzHBbBx5) and [YouTube short here](https://youtube.com/shorts/ACJDB2iQ5ko?si=SejmM_KoUiKGnS_0).

This event is a labor of love, and it would be absolutely impossible without the incredible people who give their time, talent, and support.
