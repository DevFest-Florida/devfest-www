# 🚀 Join Us at the DevFest Florida 2026 Hackathon 🎉

Central Florida tech community, the countdown is officially on! ⏳ 

In just **6 days**, we kick off the **DevFest Florida Hackathon 2026** at the **UCF Downtown Campus (Dr. Phillips Academic Commons)** in Orlando! 🌴✨ 

Whether you write code daily, design intuitive interfaces, guide product vision, or build immersive game worlds—this weekend is for you. DevFest isn’t about non-stop, sleep-deprived grinds; it’s a high-energy celebration of innovation, learning, human-centered technology, and what we can create right here in Central Florida. 🤝💡

## 🎙️ Keynote Spotlight: Larry Kilroy (VP of Technology, DataKind)

We are thrilled to welcome **Larry Kilroy**, Vice President of Technology at **DataKind**, as our 2026 Keynote Speaker! 🌍✨

With over two decades of technical leadership building high-impact data systems, Larry sits at the forefront of applying data science, cloud architectures, and machine learning to address urgent humanitarian and civic challenges. 

### Keynote Themes & What to Expect:
* 🤝 **AI & Data Science for Public Good:** Moving past tech for tech’s sake—how engineers, designers, and data professionals can address real-world community, educational, and social inequities.
* 🏗️ **Open Technology & Data Infrastructure:** Building scalable, secure, and open architectures that empower civic organizations, schools, and non-profits without vendor lock-in.
* ⚖️ **Responsible AI & Data Maturity:** Practical frameworks for deploying ethical, human-in-the-loop models that minimize bias and deliver measurable, grassroots impact.

Larry's talk sets the stage and vision for everything we're building over the weekend—especially as you brainstorm concepts for your hackathon projects!

## 🌟 Featured Speakers & Workshops: Learn from the Best

Level up your toolkit with hands-on sessions and deep dives led by top engineering leaders throughout the weekend:

- 🤖 **Arthur Thompson (Google):** *Building AI apps from scratch with the Google ecosystem* — Discover how modern builders orchestrate agents and cloud services to ship software fast.
- 📱 **Carol Bolger (Real World Applications):** *Ask My Data: Building Grounded Gemini Agents with Flutter & Firebase* — Learn hands-on how to eliminate hallucinations with live Firestore data.
- 🛠️ **Will Gorman (Nucleus Security):** *Agentic Engineering Workshop* — Dive into autonomous agent skills and the evolving software lifecycle.
- 👓 **Dwane Hemmings (Vonage):** *WebXR: Augment Reality with Real-time Communications* — Build next-gen interactive AR experiences for headsets and smart glasses.
- 📐 **Loiane Groner (BNY) & Javiera Laso (Thoughtworks):** Discover Specs-Driven Development, feedback loops, and AI-assisted Architecture Review Boards.
- 🧪 **German Gamboa (Olo):** Master testing and safety harnesses for complex agent systems.

## 🎯 Spotlight Challenge: Transforming Education & Learning 📚✨

While we have competitive tracks spanning **Devs**, **Game Devs**, and **Data for Good**, our marquee spotlight theme this year is **Education & Learning**:

- **Personalized AI Mentors & Tutors:** How can multimodal models adapt to diverse student needs and learning styles?
- **Accessible & Immersive EdTech:** Leverage WebXR and interactive media to bring complex STEM, history, or vocational concepts to life.
- **Civic & Open Learning Platforms:** Build lightweight, community-first tools that break down barriers to digital literacy and technical skill-building.

Bring your best ideas to life—with real cash prizes, sponsor perks, and direct feedback from industry mentors! 🏆💰
