Scaling Architectural Thinking with AI and Internal Standards

AI is integrated into how I approach architectural decisions. I use it alongside internal engineering standards, previous decisions, system constraints, established patterns, and organizational knowledge to accelerate analysis, explore alternatives, and expose trade-offs that deserve deeper consideration. The value comes from combining AI’s ability to synthesize and challenge with the technical and organizational context required for sound architectural judgment.

In this talk, I’ll share how I use AI in my own architecture practice: how I bring the right context into the conversation, use it to explore options and trade-offs, challenge assumptions, identify relevant standards, and document the reasoning behind a decision.

I’ll also share the mistakes and limitations I’ve encountered along the way—because AI can sound convincing even when it is missing critical context.

Most importantly, this won’t just be a walkthrough of my process. I’ll break the approach into practical patterns you can adapt to your own organization, even if your standards, tools, and architecture practice look completely different.

You’ll leave with ideas for building your own AI-assisted decision workflow while keeping engineers and architects responsible for the judgment, context, and final decision.

Ask My Data: Building Grounded Gemini Agents with Flutter & Firebase

Most AI agents lean on one deceptively simple trick to be useful: grounding. In this hands-on workshop, you'll build a Flutter chat app where Gemini answers questions using your own live Firestore data instead of guessing — the same function-calling pattern behind real agentic apps. Along the way, you'll set up a Firebase project from scratch (free, no credit card required), learn what Firestore, Authentication, and Firebase AI Logic actually do, and wire them into a working Flutter app. You'll leave with a template you can immediately re-skin and extend for your hackathon project this weekend, plus a real mental model for grounding an LLM in data instead of letting it hallucinate.
