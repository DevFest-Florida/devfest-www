## 2026 Keynote — To Be Announced

We are curating an unforgettable keynote experience for **DevFest Hackathon 2026 (November 6–8, Orlando, FL)**. Our team is in active conversation with speakers, and the full lineup will be announced here first.

**What to expect on stage:**

- Thought-provoking talks from industry leaders and community builders shaping the future of AI.
- Practical, hands-on insights on building with AI responsibly and ethically; tools, guardrails, and patterns that work in production.
- Stories from developers, designers, founders, and product leaders who are turning bold ideas into real impact.

**Who we are bringing together**

Expect a mix of returning favorites from our 2025 stage and new voices from across the Google ecosystem and beyond; Google Developer Experts, Microsoft, Vonage, GitHub, Local Community Leaders, the open-source community, and the Florida tech scene.

### Stay in the loop

Speaker announcements will roll out on this page as they are confirmed. To be notified first — or to nominate a speaker — reach the organizers at **organizers@devfestflorida.com**.
