## 2026 Speakers — Coming Soon

The DevFest Hackathon 2026 speaker lineup is being assembled and will be announced here first. From November 6–8 in Orlando, FL, we are bringing together a diverse mix of voices from across the Google ecosystem, the open-source community, and the Florida tech scene.

**Voices to expect on the 2026 stage:**

- **Engineers and architects** shipping AI agents, multi-agent systems, and production GenAI on Google Cloud, Azure, and beyond.
- **Product leaders** who can translate research into real-world product value.
- **Designers and storytellers** exploring ephemeral, AI-driven, and human-centered interfaces.
- **Community builders and educators** from GDG, Microsoft, Vonage, GitHub, and partner networks.
- **Florida founders and operators** turning regional strengths into global impact.

**Why this lineup matters**

DevFest Hackathon is built around three ideas: building with AI responsibly, forging real connections, and elevating the people behind the work. Every speaker we invite is chosen because they bring one of those threads to life on stage and in the room.
